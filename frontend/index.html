Why AuthenChain

Every Layer Built for Trust

From the moment a product is manufactured to the moment it reaches a consumer's hands, every step is logged, hashed, and verifiable.

AI Image Verification

Upload or capture a product photo and our AI engine compares it against the manufacturer's registered reference image for authenticity signals.

Blockchain Ledger

Every registration and verification event is written to a hash-linked chain, so tampering with product records is instantly detectable.

Instant QR Verification

Manufacturers generate a unique QR code per batch automatically; consumers scan it for instant, transparent verification.

Real-Time Analytics

Manufacturers and administrators get live dashboards tracking counterfeit trends, scan volumes, and risk distribution.

Counterfeit Alerts

The moment a suspicious or counterfeit scan is detected, manufacturers are notified so they can respond quickly.

Secure by Design

JWT authentication, hashed passwords, and role-based access control keep every account and product record protected.
