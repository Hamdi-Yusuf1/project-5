Our Mission

Fighting Counterfeit Skincare With Technology

AuthenChain was built as a final year BSc Information Technology project to demonstrate how AI-assisted verification and blockchain-backed record keeping can protect consumers from counterfeit skincare products.

The Problem

Counterfeit Skincare Puts Consumers at Risk

Counterfeit cosmetic products often contain unregulated or harmful ingredients, and consumers currently have no fast, reliable way to check what they're buying is genuine.

Health Risk
Brand Damage
Our Approach

Three Pillars of Trust

Simulated AI Analysis

Deterministic, explainable image-comparison scoring designed so a real trained model can be swapped in later without changing the system architecture.

Simulated Blockchain

A hash-linked ledger of registration and verification events, using SHA-256 chaining and lightweight proof-of-work — real, verifiable, and self-contained.

QR-Based Traceability

Every registered batch gets a unique, styled QR code, generated automatically and downloadable by the manufacturer.
